These generic external models are contributor-reported AI-assisted creations with separate exterior materials, not scanned/manufacturer CAD models. They are not represented as photorealistic. A suitable realistic CC0 sedan was not verified: the existing original ParkPulse sedan remains as an explicit quality limitation. Files are self-contained and have no remote decoder/texture dependency. Cars are instanced by material; no animation clips or interior objects are rendered.

parkpulse-real-compact, parkpulse-real-sedan, parkpulse-real-suv and parkpulse-real-van are original ParkPulse models created for this project. No third-party mesh or real manufacturer marks are used. The source generator is create-vehicles.mjs. Separate PBR materials preserve paint, glass, rubber, alloy, trim and lamps. Each self-contained GLB is approximately 210 KB, with no decoder or external texture dependency.
